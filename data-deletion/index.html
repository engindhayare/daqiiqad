Data deletion

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Daqiiqad is a product of Biriir, a technology business based in Garowe, Puntland, Somalia. You can ask us to delete all data we hold about you.

How

  1. Send the word DELETE (or TIRTIR) to the Daqiiqad WhatsApp number from the phone number you used.
  2. Or message Biriir on WhatsApp: +1 711 504 5234.

What happens

We delete your phone number, name, city and messages, and remove your details from past orders within 30 days. We confirm on WhatsApp when it is done. Shops may keep their own copy of a receipt if the law requires it for their records.

Tirtirka xogta

Si aad u tirtirto xogtaada, u dir erayga TIRTIR lambarka WhatsApp ee Daqiiqad, laga soo diro lambarkii aad isticmaashay. Waxaan tirtiraynaa xogtaada 30 maalmood gudahood, waana kuu xaqiijin doonnaa WhatsApp.
