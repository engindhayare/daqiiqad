Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Daqiiqad is a product of Biriir, a technology business based in Garowe, Puntland, Somalia. Daqiiqad is a WhatsApp ordering service for shops in Puntland, Somalia. Customers order from local shops by chatting with the Daqiiqad WhatsApp number. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can have it deleted. In this policy, "we" means Biriir.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and WhatsApp profile name.
  • The messages and button choices you send to the Daqiiqad number.
  • Order details: shop, products, quantity, delivery or pickup, amount, order code.
  • Your city, if you tell us, so we can show you local shops.
  • The wallet type (for example Sahal or eDahab) and the phone number you say you will pay from.
  • For shop staff: name, phone number and the shop they manage.

We never ask for or store your PIN, password, or wallet login. Payments happen in your own mobile wallet, directly to the shop. Biriir does not receive or hold your money.

Why we use it

  • To take your order and send it to the shop you chose.
  • To send you payment instructions, receipts and delivery updates.
  • So the shop can confirm your payment and contact you about delivery.
  • To prevent fraud and fix problems.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • The shop you order from: your name, number and order.
  • Meta (WhatsApp), which carries the messages under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Our hosting provider (Supabase, servers in the European Union), which stores the data for us.
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for up to 12 months so shops can handle questions and refunds, then deleted. You can ask us to delete your data sooner.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time. See Data deletion.
