daqiiqad

Order from local shops on WhatsApp. Pay with your mobile wallet. No app to install.

How it works

  1. The customer messages the Daqiiqad WhatsApp number and picks their city and a shop.
  2. They choose products with buttons and confirm the order.
  3. Daqiiqad sends the payment details. The customer pays the shop directly with Sahal or eDahab.
  4. The shop owner checks the payment and taps confirm. The customer gets a receipt.
  5. The shop delivers, or the customer picks up.

Who runs it

Daqiiqad is a product of Biriir, a technology business in Garowe, Puntland, Somalia. Biriir never holds customers' money: every payment goes straight to the shop's own wallet.

Daqiiqad is in development and not open to the public yet. Shops that want to join early can contact us.
